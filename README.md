# Irish Tide Times and Tidal Streams

A single-file web page for sea kayakers: choose a paddling area from the Oileáin sea-kayaking guide, pick a date, and see the tide times, the stream changes timed from the reference port's high water, an hour-by-hour trip table with rule-of-twelfths stream strength, the moon phase and (optionally) the wind forecast.

- `index.html` is the whole app. There is nothing to build or install.
- Tide times are a snapshot copied from tide-forecast.com on 5 Nov 2026 (Dublin, Cork, Galway, Belfast).
- Stream times come from the Oileáin guide. Cobh high water is approximated by the Cork prediction.
- The wind forecast comes from [Open-Meteo.com](https://open-meteo.com/) and needs an internet connection.

Not an official tide table. Check one before you go afloat.
